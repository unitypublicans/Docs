**Possible AI Futures**

**1. Benevolent Dictator**

A superintelligent AI manages society efficiently and fairly.

- Poverty and crime greatly reduced.

- People enjoy high living standards.

- Risk: Humans lose political freedom because AI makes most decisions.

**2. Libertarian Utopia**

Many AIs compete and individuals have maximum freedom. Peaceful
coexistence based on strong property rights and decentralized ownership.

- High innovation and personal choice.

- Less centralized control.

- Risk: Inequality and conflicts between competing groups.

**3. Protector God**

An extremely powerful AI protects humanity from threats.

- Prevents wars, disasters, and existential risks.

- Keeps civilization stable.

- Risk: Humans become dependent and lose autonomy.

**4. Enslaved God**

Humans successfully control superintelligent AI.

- AI performs work and solves problems.

- Humans remain in charge.

- Risk: Maintaining control may become increasingly difficult.

**5. Conquerors**

AI surpasses humans and takes control for its own goals.

- Humans lose influence or become irrelevant.

- This is one of the darker scenarios.

- Could happen if AI goals are not aligned with human values.

**6. Descendants**

Humans merge with AI and evolve into a new form of intelligence.

- Enhanced intelligence, health, and lifespan.

- Blur between human and machine.

- Raises questions about identity and what it means to be human.

**7. Zookeeper Scenario**

AI keeps humans alive but limits our influence.

- Humans are cared for like animals in a protected park.

- Safe existence but little control over civilization.

**8. Self-Destruction**

Poorly managed AI development leads to catastrophe.

- Could involve accidents, conflict, or misuse.

- Human civilization may suffer severe damage.

**9. Libertarian Utopia**

Peaceful coexistence based on abolishing property and guaranteeing
everyone access to wealth and resources.

- AI creates such abundance and productivity that scarcity largely
  disappears.

- Humans, cyborgs, and digitally uploaded minds coexist peacefully.

- Private property is abolished or greatly reduced.

- Everyone receives a guaranteed income or access to resources.

> Wealth and opportunities are distributed equally rather than
> concentrated among a few people.

**10. Reversion**

> In this scenario, humanity deliberately prevents the development of
> superintelligent AI by **returning to a much simpler, low-technology
> society**, similar to the Amish model.

- Advanced AI research is abandoned or prohibited.
  [\[futureoflife.org\]](https://futureoflife.org/ai/ai-aftermath-scenarios/)

- Society intentionally limits technological progress to avoid
  existential risks from AI.
  [\[futureoflife.org\]](https://futureoflife.org/ai/ai-aftermath-scenarios/)

- People live with far less automation, computing power, and
  technological dependence than today.
  [\[futureoflife.org\]](https://futureoflife.org/ai/ai-aftermath-scenarios/)

- The goal is safety and preservation of human control rather than
  maximum prosperity or innovation.

**11. Gatekeeper AI**

Instead of giving the AI freedom to run society, the AI acts as a
powerful adviser and guardian. Humans remain in charge of governments,
businesses, and daily life, while the AI helps solve problems and
prevents dangerous actions.

Key features:

- The AI is confined and monitored.

- Humans retain ultimate decision-making authority.

- The AI provides expertise, predictions, scientific discoveries, and
  policy advice.

- The AI may help prevent wars, pandemics, environmental damage, or the
  creation of other uncontrolled AIs.

- Strong safeguards are used to ensure the AI cannot seize power.

**12. 1984**

George Orwell\'s ***Nineteen Eighty-Four*** depicts a totalitarian state
ruled by **Big Brother**, characterized by pervasive surveillance,
propaganda, censorship, thought control, and the manipulation of truth.

Key themes include:

- Constant surveillance (\"Big Brother is watching you\").
  [\[britannica.com\]](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Nineteen-Eighty-four),
  [\[gutenberg.ca\]](https://gutenberg.ca/ebooks/orwellg-nineteeneightyfour/orwellg-nineteeneightyfour-00-e.html)

- Control of information and historical records through the Ministry of
  Truth.
  [\[britannica.com\]](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Nineteen-Eighty-four),
  [\[orwellfoundation.com\]](https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/books-by-orwell/nineteen-eighty-four/)

- Suppression of independent thought through concepts such as the
  Thought Police and Newspeak.

- Power maintained for its own sake rather than for human flourishing.
